# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- The `cctop` command, with `cctop watch` and `cctop open`, ready to be linked as a cornercase plugin.
- Light at rest: the process sample reads little per process and cornercase is asked for its panes every 10 s or when a new pane shows up, so an agent's status can take up to 10 s to change in cctop.
